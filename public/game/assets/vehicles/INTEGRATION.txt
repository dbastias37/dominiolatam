DOMINIO VEHICLE ASSET CONTRACT

Copy this vehicles directory into the Site's dist/assets/vehicles directory.
No Blender runtime, Python, textures, Draco decoder or remote URLs are required by the game.

manifest.json has tanks and jets arrays with id, name, file, type, originalGrouping,
triangles, bytes, dimensions, front, up, length and source.

All assets use glTF +Y up and -Z forward, longitudinal length 1. Their root is
centered horizontally and sits at minimum Y=0. Apply your normal game unit scale
at the outer Object3D; no extra 90-degree corrective rotation is needed.

Tanks contain an empty Object3D named muzzle centered on the actual dark barrel
opening polygons. The ZSU-57-2 also has muzzle_2. Local -Z points along the bore.
Object3D.getWorldDirection returns local +Z, so negate it or rotate (0,0,-1) by
the marker world quaternion. Use getWorldPosition for the projectile origin.
The root-space fallback position/direction is also in the manifest.

The turretNode field gives the movable turret root name. Gun and muzzle descend
from it. Kanonenjagdpanzer has a fixed gun and no turret node. M163, Type 80 and
ZSU-57-2 preserve the author's small/upward barrel inclinations.

Aircraft fuselages, both wings and any supplied multipart geometry are combined
correctly. A nose node marks the nose extremum. Models contain no animation clips;
translate and rotate the outer instance along the route tangent during gameplay.

Source material colors are preserved (including blue cockpit glazing) as
texture-free flat PBR paints. Clone materials before applying nation-specific
color accents to instances to avoid recoloring other cached models.

Centinela and Guardian are fictional game aliases for two unnamed original
source meshes. The models are game choices, not claims about national inventories.
A-4E/A-4M and F-111/EF-111 are related variants supplied by the artist; labels and
nation markings should distinguish these related silhouettes in the game.

Credits and source links are in ATTRIBUTION.txt. Original blend files are unchanged.
